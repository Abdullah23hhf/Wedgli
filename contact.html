Contact us

Questions, feedback, or a small space you'd like us to cover? Reach out any time — we read every message.

Email us directly

The fastest way to reach Wedgli is by email. Tap the button below and your mail app will open with the address already filled in.

Email Hisabify.online@gmail.com

What to include in your message

A little detail helps us reply faster and more usefully:

  • The room or space you're trying to organize (studio, rental, dorm, shared apartment, and so on)
  • Any constraints — no drilling, no permanent changes, tight budget, limited square footage
  • A photo, if you have one — it's the quickest way for us to understand the space

Response time

We typically reply within a few business days. If your message is time-sensitive, mention that in the subject line and we'll try to prioritize it.

Other ways to reach us

  • Guide requests: tell us which space or problem you'd like us to cover next
  • Corrections: spotted something inaccurate in a guide? Let us know and we'll fix it
  • Partnerships: brands and small businesses can also reach us at the email above

Frequently asked

Do you offer one-on-one organizing consultations?
Not at the moment — Wedgli is currently a guides-only site, but it's something we may add in the future.

Can I suggest a product for you to review?
Yes. Send the product name and where you found it, and we'll take a look for an upcoming guide.
