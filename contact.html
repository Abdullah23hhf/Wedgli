Contact us

Questions, feedback, or a topic you'd like us to cover? Reach out any time. We do our best to read every message.

Email us directly

The fastest way to reach Wedgli is by email. Tap the button below and your mail app will open with the address already filled in.

Email help.wedgli.com@gmail.com

What to include in your message

A little detail helps us reply faster and more usefully:

  • The topic or space you have in mind (for example, a studio kitchen, a shared apartment, or a student offer)
  • Any constraints, such as no drilling, no permanent changes, a tight budget, or limited square footage
  • A photo, if you have one. It's the quickest way for us to understand a space.

Response time

We usually reply within a few business days. If your message is time-sensitive, mention that in the subject line and we'll try to prioritize it.

Other ways to reach us

  • Topic requests: tell us which space, problem, or everyday question you'd like us to cover next.
  • Corrections: spotted an outdated price, offer, or other mistake? Send us the page link and we'll check the official source and fix it.
  • Partnerships: brands and small businesses can also reach us at the email above. Any sponsored content will be clearly labeled.

Frequently asked

Do you offer one-on-one organizing consultations?
Not at the moment. Wedgli is currently a guides-only site, but we may add this in the future.

Can I suggest a product for you to review?
Yes. Send the product name and where you found it, and we'll take a look for an upcoming guide.
