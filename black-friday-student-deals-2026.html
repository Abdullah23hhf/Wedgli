Black Friday 2026 Student Deals: Verified Offers, Dates & What to Wait For

Published: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026 · For US college students

Quick answer

  • Black Friday is Nov 27, 2026 (Cyber Monday is Nov 30). Retailers have not published their full 2026 lineups yet, so this guide sticks to offers we could confirm.
  • Don't wait on the two biggest AI offers. ChatGPT's 4 free months must be claimed by Oct 31, and Google's free year of AI Pro by Dec 31.
  • Wait for Black Friday on laptops (most non-Apple models), unless you want a MacBook, which usually costs less in back-to-school season or at Costco.
  • Every free trial below renews into a paid plan unless you cancel. Put the cancel date in your calendar the day you sign up.

Student deals to claim now (they don't need Black Friday)

Most student pricing is year-round or tied to a fixed deadline, so Black Friday does not usually improve it. Claim these on their own schedule.

  • Free, no deadline: GitHub Student Developer Pack (Copilot Student and GitHub Pro) and the Notion Education plan stay free while you are a verified student. Notion needs a school email.
  • Free with a deadline: ChatGPT Plus (4 months, claim by Oct 31) and Google AI Pro (1 year, redeem by Dec 31). Details in the section below.
  • Half price, not free: Perplexity Education Pro ($9/month billed annually), Microsoft 365 Personal ($4.99/month) and Grammarly Pro (50% off, limited time).
  • Music and video: Spotify Student and Apple Music Student are both $6.99/month after a free first month. See the streaming section.

The two AI deals with deadlines

Google AI Pro: free for a year (redeem by Dec 31)

Google's student page lists a free one-year Google AI Pro plan for eligible US college students aged 18 and over, including 5 TB of storage and higher Gemini usage limits. Eligibility has to be verified each year, and new users and students whose 2025 trial has run out can qualify. A valid payment method is required at sign-up, and the plan switches to $19.99/month after the year unless you cancel.

Because the redeem-by date is December 31, you gain nothing by waiting for Black Friday. Claim it when you actually need it, but not later than the deadline.

ChatGPT Plus: four free months (claim by Oct 31)

OpenAI's student page offers four free months of ChatGPT Plus to current full-time and part-time students at eligible US degree-granting schools. SheerID checks your enrollment, and you need a school-issued email for that step, though your ChatGPT account can use a different email. You will not be charged during the free months, but it renews at $20/month afterward. If you cancel early, you keep access until the end of the current billing period but lose the remaining free months. Subscriptions billed through the App Store or Google Play are not supported.

Our take: if you will use only one, Google's free year is the better value because it lasts longer. If you want both, claim ChatGPT first since it expires sooner.

Laptops: buy now or wait for Black Friday?

According to ShopBack's laptop timing guide, late November is the cheapest month for most US laptop buyers, with roughly 10 to 30% off most non-Apple models and the deepest cuts on Chromebooks and mid-range Windows laptops. It also warns that popular configurations can sell out within hours.

  • Windows laptops and Chromebooks: Black Friday and Cyber Monday are usually the best window. Decide your budget and specs in advance so you can buy fast.
  • MacBooks: The same guide says June through September (back-to-school), plus Costco year-round, tends to beat Black Friday. Apple's education store lists discounted Mac and iPad pricing for students, but it shows no end date and says promotions can end at any time.
  • If your laptop is dying now: Student back-to-school sales can match Black Friday pricing, so a fall purchase is not necessarily a bad one. If you can limp through to Nov 27, waiting usually pays.

Music and streaming for students

Student plans are already about half the price of regular plans, and Black Friday rarely changes that. Spotify's student plan is $6.99/month after a one-month free trial and includes Hulu with ads (subject to eligibility). You must be 18 or older at an eligible US college, and verification happens through SheerID, repeated every 12 months. Apple Music's student plan is also $6.99/month with a free first month, and it includes Apple TV for verified college students. Pick one music service, not both.

A simple plan before Black Friday

  1. This week: Claim ChatGPT before Oct 31 if you will use it. Set a calendar reminder for the week before the free months end.
  2. By Dec 31: Redeem the Google AI Pro year. Add a cancel reminder for 11 months later.
  3. Now: Pick your laptop model and budget. Put it in your cart's wishlist so you can buy quickly on Nov 27.
  4. Black Friday week: Compare prices on the same model across stores, and check the return window before buying.
  5. Anytime: Switch on one student music plan and one productivity tool, not five.

Mistakes that cost students money

  • Forgetting auto-renewal. Both ChatGPT and Google AI Pro charge you after the free period unless you cancel.
  • Using the wrong account. Some offers do not work on a school-managed account, and app-store subscriptions are not supported for ChatGPT's offer.
  • Buying from "student discount" sites you don't recognize. Use the company's own page. Nobody sells an official student plan at a mysterious flat rate.
  • Waiting on a deal that has a deadline. Black Friday does not extend Oct 31 or Dec 31.
  • Buying a laptop on impulse. A big percentage off a weak spec is still a weak laptop. Check the processor, RAM and storage first.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, the day after Thanksgiving (November 26). Cyber Monday is November 30.

Should college students wait for Black Friday to buy a laptop?

For most non-Apple laptops, usually yes. For MacBooks, back-to-school season or Costco tends to be cheaper. Popular models can sell out within hours, so plan ahead.

Do student AI deals get better on Black Friday?

Not that we can confirm. ChatGPT's offer must be claimed by October 31 and Google's by December 31, so waiting risks missing them.

Is Google AI Pro really free for US college students?

Yes, for eligible students aged 18 or older who redeem by December 31, 2026. A payment method is required at sign-up, and it charges $19.99/month after the free year unless you cancel.

Will I be charged after a free student trial ends?

Usually yes. ChatGPT renews at $20/month and Google AI Pro at $19.99/month after the free period unless you cancel first. Set a reminder before it ends.

Do I need a .edu email to get student discounts?

Often a school-issued email is needed for verification (ChatGPT and Notion, for example), but other services accept different proof through SheerID, and the account you use can differ from the school email.
