Black Friday 2026 Student Deals: What to Claim, What to Wait For

By: Abdullah · Published: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026 · For US college students

Quick answer

  • Black Friday is Nov 27, 2026 (Cyber Monday is Nov 30). Some retailers start early: Target's sale runs Nov 5 to 7, and Best Buy and Walmart follow later this month. Full dates are in the sale calendar below.
  • The ChatGPT student offer is over. It closed on Oct 31 and has not been extended. Google's free year of AI Pro is still open until Dec 31.
  • Wait for Black Friday on most non-Apple laptops. MacBooks usually cost less in back-to-school season or at Costco.
  • Every free trial renews into a paid plan unless you cancel. Put the cancel date in your calendar the day you sign up.

Student deals come in two kinds: ones with a deadline, and ones that never really end. Black Friday only changes the second group a little, mostly on laptops and other tech. This guide covers what is still open, what has already ended, when the big retailers start, and the mistakes that cost students money. Everything here is for US college students, in US dollars, and each row says when we checked it.

Student deals to claim now (they don't need Black Friday)

Most student pricing is year-round or tied to a fixed deadline, so Black Friday does not usually improve it. Claim these on their own schedule.

  • Free, no deadline: GitHub Student Developer Pack (including Copilot Student) and the Notion Education plan stay free while you are a verified student. Notion needs a school email.
  • Free with a deadline: Google AI Pro (1 year, redeem by Dec 31). The ChatGPT offer has ended, see the AI section below.
  • Half price, not free: Perplexity Education Pro ($10/month), Microsoft 365 Personal ($4.99/month) and Grammarly Pro (50% off, limited time).
  • Music and video: Spotify Student and Apple Music Student are both $6.99/month after a free first month. Peacock has a student rate too. See the streaming section.

AI deals: Google is open, ChatGPT has ended

Google AI Pro: free for a year (redeem by Dec 31)

Google's student page lists a free one-year Google AI Pro plan for eligible US college students aged 18 and over. It includes 5 TB of storage and higher Gemini usage limits. Eligibility has to be verified each year, and both new users and students whose 2025 trial has run out can qualify. You need a personal Google account (school-issued accounts are not supported) and a valid payment method at sign-up. After the year, the plan switches to $19.99/month unless you cancel.

Black Friday will not improve this offer, so there is no reason to wait for it. Claim it when you will actually use it, and before Dec 31.

ChatGPT Plus: the free-months offer ended on Oct 31

OpenAI's Back to School offer gave eligible US students four free months of ChatGPT Plus, but the claim window closed on October 31, 2026. As of November 5, OpenAI's help page still lists that deadline and mentions no extension, so you can no longer claim it.

If you did claim in time, your four free months run from your own claim date. After that, Plus renews at $20/month unless you cancel. Cancelling early means you lose the remaining free months, though you keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Missed ChatGPT? What to claim instead

  • Google AI Pro: the best free option right now, a full year at no cost.
  • Perplexity Education Pro: $10/month, an AI search and research tool with student pricing.
  • Microsoft 365 Personal: $4.99/month, with Copilot built into Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
  • GitHub Copilot Student: free for verified students, mainly useful if you write code.
  • Notion Education: free Plus plan for notes and study planning.

Our take: if you missed ChatGPT, claim Google's free year first. It is the only big free AI offer still open, and it runs for a full 12 months from the day you redeem it.

Laptops: buy now or wait for Black Friday?

ShopBack's laptop timing guide says November is the cheapest month to buy a laptop in the US, and that buying at the wrong time can leave 15 to 30% on the table. Its advice depends on what you are buying:

  • Windows laptops and Chromebooks: Black Friday and Cyber Monday are usually the best window. Pick your model and budget in advance, because popular models can sell out.
  • MacBooks: ShopBack says June through September (back-to-school), plus Costco year-round, tends to beat Black Friday. Apple's Back to School gift card offer ended on September 24. Apple's regular education pricing on Mac and iPad is a separate program.
  • If your laptop is dying now: back-to-school sales can match Black Friday pricing, so a fall purchase is not necessarily a bad one. If you can hold out until Nov 27, waiting usually pays.

Stack your student discount on top

Dell gives students an extra 10% off. You sign in to Dell.com, register for Dell Rewards and verify through SheerID. You do not need a school email, since documents like a student ID can work. The discount only applies to purchases made directly on Dell.com, and Dell says exclusions apply, so check your cart to see whether it stacks with a Black Friday price.

HP, Lenovo and Samsung also run student stores, but the percentages quoted online vary a lot from source to source. We are not listing numbers until we can confirm them on the brands' own pages, so check each student store before you check out.

Software watch list: Adobe has often discounted Creative Cloud around Black Friday, including for students. As of November 5 we could not find an official 2026 announcement, so we are not listing a price. We will add it once Adobe publishes one.

Music and streaming for students

Student plans are already about half the regular price, and Black Friday rarely changes that.

  • Spotify Premium Student: $6.99/month after a one-month free trial, with Hulu (with ads) included, subject to eligibility. You must be 18 or older at an eligible US college. Verification runs through SheerID and repeats every 12 months, for up to four years in total.
  • Apple Music Student: $6.99/month after a free first month for new subscribers. Apple TV is included for verified college students, but Apple calls that a limited-time offer that can end at any time.
  • Peacock Student: $5.99/month for 12 months on the ad-supported Premium plan, verified through SheerID. After 12 months you pay the regular price unless you re-verify.

Pick one music service, not both. Hulu, Paramount+ and YouTube Premium also have student plans, but their prices change often, so check each official page before you sign up.

How student verification works

Most offers use a verification service, and the rules differ more than you would expect. Here is what each official page says:

  • SheerID is used by Google AI Pro, Spotify, Peacock, Grammarly, Perplexity and Dell. For ChatGPT it could use enrollment records, school single sign-on or documents.
  • School email required: Notion, and it must be the main email on your account. Notion does not accept a student ID.
  • Personal account required: Google AI Pro and Microsoft 365 need a personal account rather than a school-issued one. Microsoft then checks your student status with a school email or other documents.
  • No school email needed: Dell accepts documents such as a student ID, class schedule or tuition receipt.
  • GitHub verifies you through GitHub Education and rechecks your eligibility every month.

Enter your name exactly as it appears on your documents, or verification can fail.

A simple plan before Black Friday

  1. Now: Redeem Google AI Pro if you will use it, and add a cancel reminder for 11 months from now.
  2. This week: Pick your laptop model and budget. Verify your student status with the stores you plan to use (Dell, for example) so you are ready to buy.
  3. Nov 5 to 19: Watch the early sales at Target, Best Buy and Walmart, and compare the same model across stores.
  4. Black Friday week (Nov 23 to 30): Check the return window before buying, and see whether your student discount stacks with the sale price.
  5. Anytime: Switch on one student music plan and one productivity tool, not five.

Mistakes that cost students money

  • Forgetting auto-renewal. Google AI Pro and ChatGPT both charge you after the free period unless you cancel.
  • Using the wrong account. Google and Microsoft want a personal account, not a school-managed one. Store-billed subscriptions (App Store or Google Play) were not supported for ChatGPT's offer.
  • Assuming Black Friday extends a deadline. It does not. ChatGPT's offer already closed, and Google's still ends on Dec 31.
  • Buying from "student discount" sites you don't recognize. Use the company's own page. Nobody sells an official student plan at a mysterious flat rate.
  • Buying a laptop on impulse. A big percentage off a weak spec is still a weak laptop. Check the processor, RAM and storage first.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, the day after Thanksgiving (November 26). Cyber Monday is November 30.

When do Black Friday 2026 sales start?

Several retailers start early. Target's early sale runs November 5 to 7. Best Buy has early deals from November 6 and its main Black Friday sale runs November 19 to 28. Walmart has early deals November 12 to 15 and its main event November 23 to 29. Costco's sale starts November 26. Amazon had not announced its dates as of November 5.

Is the ChatGPT student offer still available?

No. OpenAI's Back to School offer of four free months of ChatGPT Plus had to be claimed by October 31, 2026. OpenAI's help page still lists that deadline and mentions no extension. Students who claimed in time keep their free months, which run from the claim date.

Is Google AI Pro really free for US college students?

Yes, for eligible US college students aged 18 or older. You get one year free if you redeem by December 31, 2026. A payment method is required at sign-up, and it charges $19.99/month after the free year unless you cancel.

Should college students wait for Black Friday to buy a laptop?

For most non-Apple laptops, usually yes, because late November is typically the cheapest month. Back-to-school sales can match it, and MacBooks tend to be cheaper from June to September or at Costco. Popular models can sell out, so pick your model and budget in advance.

Do student AI deals get better on Black Friday?

Not that we can confirm. Google's free year of AI Pro has its own December 31 deadline, and ChatGPT's offer has already closed. Black Friday does not extend either one.

Will I be charged after a free student trial ends?

Usually yes. Google AI Pro renews at $19.99/month after the free year, and ChatGPT Plus renews at $20/month after the free months, unless you cancel first. Set a reminder before the free period ends.

Do I need a .edu email to get student discounts?

Not always. Notion requires a school email as the main email on your account. Dell, ChatGPT and several others verify through SheerID, which can use enrollment records, school sign-in or documents such as a student ID. Google and Microsoft want a personal account rather than a school-issued one.
