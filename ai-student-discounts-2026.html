AI Student Discounts & Free Offers That Still Work in 2026 (US Students)

By: Abdullah · Published: October 18, 2026
Last updated: November 8, 2026

Quick answer

  • Google gives US college students aged 18+ a free year of Google AI Pro (redeem by Dec 31, 2026, then $19.99/month unless you cancel). Check one.google.com/ai-student for the current free plan before you sign up.
  • The ChatGPT student offer is over. The four free months of ChatGPT Plus had to be claimed by Oct 31, 2026, and OpenAI has not extended it.
  • GitHub Copilot Student and GitHub Pro are free while you're a verified student. GitHub paused new Copilot Student sign-ups in April and has been reopening them gradually.
  • Notion's Education plan is free with a school email.
  • Discounts only: Perplexity Education Pro ($10/mo), Microsoft 365 Personal (50% off, $4.99/mo), Grammarly Pro (50% off for students, limited time).
  • Nothing for college students: Claude has no individual student discount, and Canva Education is K-12 only.

Shopping for Black Friday? See our Black Friday 2026 student deals guide for sale dates and what to wait for.

Student offers change fast, and plenty of "student discount" pages online are stale or wrong. This guide works from each company's own student page where possible, and it leaves out anything we could not confirm.

Wedgli is not affiliated with any of the companies listed here. Prices and offers can change at any time, so always confirm on the official page before you pay.

All prices in this guide are in US dollars, checked on November 5, 2026. Prices can differ by country and change without notice.

ChatGPT (OpenAI): the student offer ended

What it was: Four free months of ChatGPT Plus, which OpenAI's offer page values at $80. OpenAI says it included ChatGPT Work and other Plus features.

Status: Students had to claim it by October 31, 2026. As of November 5, OpenAI's help page still lists that deadline and mentions no extension, so you can no longer claim it.

If you claimed in time:

  • Your four free months run from your claim date.
  • After that, Plus renews at $20/month unless you cancel.
  • If you cancel early, you keep Plus until the end of the current billing period but lose the remaining free months.
  • The offer applies to the ChatGPT account you were signed in to when you claimed it. The email you used for SheerID could be different from your ChatGPT account email.

Missed it? Claim Google's free year of AI Pro first. It is the only big free AI plan still open to US students, and it runs for 12 months from the day you redeem it. For a cheaper paid option, see Perplexity Education Pro.

Official page: OpenAI Help Center: ChatGPT Back to School offer

Claude (Anthropic)

What you get: A Free plan at $0, a Pro plan at $17/month billed annually ($20/month billed monthly), and a Max plan from $100/month. Pro adds more usage and access to Claude Code and Cowork.

Who qualifies: Anyone can use Free. Anthropic's pricing page shows no student tier for individuals. It does describe an Education plan that universities buy for students, faculty and staff.

How to claim: There's nothing to claim individually. Ask your IT office or library whether your school has a Claude plan, then sign in the way they tell you.

The catch: Any site selling a "Claude student plan" at a special flat rate isn't from Anthropic.

Best for: Free-plan users who want a solid writing and study assistant at no cost.

Official page: claude.com/pricing

Gemini (Google)

What you get: A free 12-month Google AI Pro plan for eligible US college students aged 18+. Students in many other countries get Google AI Plus instead.

Who qualifies: College students in eligible countries, with a personal Google Account. Eligibility is shown on the official student page.

How to claim:

  1. Open one.google.com/ai-student on a personal account.
  2. Choose the student offer and verify with SheerID.
  3. Add a payment method at sign-up.

The catch:

  • Redeem by December 31, 2026. The 12 months run from the day you redeem.
  • It auto-charges after the free period unless you cancel. Google AI Pro is normally $19.99/month, so check the price shown when you redeem.
  • It doesn't work on a school-issued Workspace account.

Best for: Heavy researchers and anyone living in Google Docs, Gmail and Drive.

Official page: one.google.com/ai-student

NotebookLM, now Gemini Notebook (Google)

What you get: A free source-grounded research notebook. Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in July 2026.

Who qualifies: Anyone with a Google account. Paid tiers are bundled into Google AI plans, so the student AI plan lifts your limits.

How to claim: Sign in with a personal Google account and open a notebook. For higher limits, claim the Gemini offer above.

What the student plans add: Google's student offer page describes the extra room in Gemini Notebook like this: the Google AI Plus student plan lets you analyse up to 200 pages of class notes, and the Google AI Pro student plan up to 1,500 pages.

The catch: Exact free and paid caps change. Check Gemini Notebook Help for current numbers. Google shows different student offers by country. Seen from Pakistan on November 8, the page listed Google AI Plus free for 12 months and Google AI Pro at 75% off for up to 4 years, not a free year of AI Pro. Open the page from your own country to see what you actually get.

Best for: Turning lecture notes, PDFs and readings into study guides and summaries.

Official page: Gemini Notebook Help

Perplexity

What you get: The Education Pro plan at $10/month, which is about half the regular Pro price. Perplexity doesn't publish an annual student price, so confirm what you're charged at checkout. The regular personal Pro plan is $17/month billed annually, and Max is $167/month billed annually ($200/month billed monthly).

Who qualifies: Verified students and educators, verified through SheerID. On the plans screen, choose "Verify as student" or "Verify as educator".

How to claim: Go to perplexity.ai/students, open the plans screen, pick the Education tab, and follow the verification flow.

The catch: It's a discount, not a free year.

Best for: Citation-heavy research months. The free plan is fine otherwise.

Official page: perplexity.ai/students

GitHub Copilot

What you get: Copilot Student through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. The same pack includes free GitHub Pro access and 180 monthly Codespaces hours while you're a student. Copilot Student is its own plan, not the paid Copilot Pro. Reports on the plan describe unlimited code completions plus a monthly allowance of 200 AI credits.

Who qualifies: Verified students. Third-party guides say a school email or enrollment documents work.

How to claim:

  1. Go to education.github.com/pack.
  2. Click "Sign up for Student Developer Pack."
  3. Sign in to GitHub and verify your student status.
  4. Turn on Copilot in your GitHub settings.

The catch:

  • Usage limits apply, and Copilot Student ends if you stop being a verified student.
  • GitHub paused new Copilot Student sign-ups on April 20, 2026 and began reopening them gradually from June 17. If Copilot still shows as the Free plan after you're verified, check your GitHub Education page.

Best for: CS and engineering students who code in VS Code or similar editors.

Official page: education.github.com/pack

Microsoft Copilot (Microsoft 365 Personal)

What you get: 50% off Microsoft 365 Personal, listed at $4.99/month. It includes Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote and Outlook, plus 1 TB of OneDrive. The student page doesn't show the regular price, so check it at sign-up.

Who qualifies: Full- and part-time students at accredited colleges. Use a personal Microsoft account, not an EDU account.

How to claim:

  1. Sign in with a personal Microsoft account (not an EDU account).
  2. Add payment info.
  3. Verify your student status with a school email or other student proof.
  4. Start using Microsoft 365 on your devices.

The catch: The subscription renews at the discounted monthly price unless you cancel. AI usage limits apply.

Best for: Students who need Office apps and Copilot together.

Official page: microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/college-student-pricing

Canva

What you get: Nothing special for college students. Canva says Education is free for K-12 teachers and students, and it isn't currently available to college and university students. You can still use the free Canva plan.

Official page: canva.com/education

Notion (Education plan)

What you get: A free Education plan for individual students. It's a free Plus plan for a one-member workspace, with up to 100 guests, unlimited uploads and 30-day page history.

Who qualifies: Students at accredited schools listed in the World Higher Education Database. You need a school email. Notion doesn't accept student IDs.

How to claim:

  1. Make your school email the main email on your Notion account.
  2. Open Settings, then Upgrade plan, in the desktop or web app (not mobile).
  3. Choose "Get free Education Plan."

The catch:

  • Each school email can claim it once.
  • You re-verify yearly.
  • Lose your school email and the workspace drops to Free.
  • If your workspace is already on a paid plan, downgrade it to Free first, then claim the Education plan.

Best for: Notes, planners and class dashboards.

Official page: notion.com/help/notion-for-education

Grammarly

What you get: For a limited time, 50% off Grammarly Pro through SheerID. Pro includes the Superhuman Go assistant, which can help with research, drafting, citations and AI and plagiarism checks. Grammarly's regular Pro price is $12/month billed annually ($144/year), or $30/month billed monthly.

Who qualifies: Students verified through SheerID, under Grammarly for Students or Grammarly for Graduate Students.

How to claim: Use the SheerID links on Grammarly's student discount support page.

The catch: The discount covers one subscription period only. It can't be combined with other offers or applied through the App Store or Google Play. The page doesn't show an end date, so check it before you sign up. Extra seats are billed at the standard rate.

Best for: Heavy writers who want writing help plus citation and plagiarism checks.

Official page: support.grammarly.com ("Does Grammarly offer discounts for students?")

Offers that ended or don't exist (don't waste your time)

  • ChatGPT's four free months for students: It closed on Oct 31, 2026 and has not been extended. See the ChatGPT section above.
  • Microsoft's free 12-month Microsoft 365 Personal for students: It ended Oct 31, 2025, according to Microsoft's support answer. Any later free promo is not confirmed.
  • The old Gemini free year: That earlier offer has closed. Google launched a new student offer on Aug 19, 2026 (see above).
  • A permanent free ChatGPT Plus program: No such program was found. The only student offer was the four-month Back to School promo, and it has closed.
  • A Claude student discount: It doesn't exist for individuals.
  • Canva Education for college students: Not available.
  • A universal free year of Perplexity Pro: the personal Pro plan is $17/month when billed annually, and the student plan is about half the regular price.

Limited or region-specific offers

  • Google AI Plus (not Pro) is the free-year plan for students in many other countries. Not for US students.
  • Grammarly, Microsoft, Notion and Claude may reach you free through your school's license. Check your library or IT page.

Common mistakes and scams

  • Fake discount sites. Go straight to the company's own page. Be suspicious of any site selling a "Claude student plan" at a flat special rate.
  • Sharing your .edu email. Offers are tied to you. Notion lets each school email claim once.
  • "Free Plus" from unofficial sellers. The official ChatGPT student offer has closed, so anyone selling "student ChatGPT Plus" now is not offering it through OpenAI. Buying a shared or resold account risks losing it.
  • Forgetting auto-renewal. Google AI Pro bills you after the free year, and ChatGPT Plus does too if you claimed it in time, unless you cancel.
  • Using a school Workspace account. Google's offer isn't valid on those.

Academic integrity

Having a tool doesn't mean you can use it in every class. Read each course syllabus and ask your instructor if the policy is unclear. When AI use is allowed, keep your drafts and notes.

Student offer not working? Common fixes

Many students get an "unavailable" message when they try to claim the Google student offer. In a Google One Help Community thread titled "Notebook LM student discount", a Google product expert listed the rules behind most of these errors:

  • Use a personal Gmail account. The offer can only be subscribed to with a personal Google account. The school (EDU) email is used for verification only.
  • One school email, one Google account. A verified EDU address can be used on only one personal account, and it stays linked to that account permanently.
  • The plan can't be shared. Google subscriptions are non-transferable, and the student plan can't be shared with a family group.
  • Cancel an existing Google One plan first. If you already pay for Google One, cancel it and wait for it to expire before you claim the student offer.
  • Sign out of all other Google accounts before you start, then sign in with only the account you want to subscribe with.
  • Check your age and country. The birthdate on your Google account must meet the age rule, and the offer has to be available in your country.
  • Not in a family group. Being a member of a family group can block the offer.

Source: Google One Help Community answer by a Google product expert, posted Oct 13, 2025. Community answers may not reflect the latest rules, so check Google's own student page too.

FAQ

Is ChatGPT free for students?

Not anymore. OpenAI's four-month ChatGPT Plus student offer had to be claimed by October 31, 2026, and it was not extended. If you claimed it in time, the free months run from your claim date, and Plus renews at $20/month unless you cancel.

Does Claude have a student discount?

Not for individuals. The Free plan is available to everyone, and some schools buy campus plans.

Is Gemini free for students?

Yes, for eligible US college students aged 18+: Google AI Pro is free for 12 months if you redeem by December 31, 2026. Students in many other countries get Google AI Plus instead.

Is NotebookLM free?

Yes, the base tier is free. Google renamed it Gemini Notebook, and paid limits come with Google AI plans.

Why does the NotebookLM student discount say "unavailable"?

Usually because of the account. The offer needs a personal Google account (not a school one), a school email that hasn't been used on another account, no existing Google One plan, and no family group. See the fixes above.

Is GitHub Copilot free for students?

Yes, Copilot Student is free for verified students through the Student Developer Pack, with usage limits. GitHub paused new Copilot Student sign-ups in April 2026 and has been reopening them gradually, so check your GitHub Education page.

Is Perplexity free for students?

Not free. For verified students, Education Pro is $10/month, about half the regular Pro price.

Do I need an .edu email?

Not always. Notion requires a school email as the main email on your account. Google AI Pro and Microsoft 365 need a personal account, and services that verify through SheerID can often use enrollment records, school sign-in or documents instead.

Is Canva free for college students?

Canva Education isn't open to them. The free Canva plan is.

Will I be charged after the free trial?

Yes for Google AI Pro, and for ChatGPT Plus if you claimed it in time, unless you cancel first. Both need a payment method.

Conclusion

Right now, the strongest moves are Google's free year of AI Pro (redeem by Dec 31), GitHub Copilot Student, and Notion's free Education plan. The ChatGPT student offer has closed, so Perplexity Education Pro and Microsoft 365 Personal are the best paid options. Student offers change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.
