Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information when you visit Wedgli.

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wedgli ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit wedgli.com (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information described here.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account to read the Site. We may collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you provide: your name and email address, if you contact us directly through our contact page or email.
  • Automatically collected information: like most websites, our server and third-party services may log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages visited.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your questions, comments, or requests
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the Site

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. It may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Cookies and advertising

We may display advertising on the Site in the future, including through Google AdSense. If we do, Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

If we show ads from other partners, they may also use technologies such as web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage in connection with ad delivery and measurement.

Third-party services

Besides analytics, we may use other third-party services to run the Site. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they use data.

Links to other sites

The Site links to other websites, including the official sites of the companies and products we write about, and any affiliate links we add in the future will be labeled as such (see our Disclaimer for details). Clicking these links may allow the other site or a retailer to place its own cookies on your device. Their privacy policies apply once you leave our Site, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Data retention

We retain information you send us for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or as required by law. Automatically collected log data is generally retained only as long as necessary for security and site-performance purposes.

Your choices

  • You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with the add-on linked above, and of personalized ads with the links above.
  • You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to request it be deleted.

Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
