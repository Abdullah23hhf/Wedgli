About Wedgli

Practical guides for everyday life, written for people who rent, live small, and don't have a garage to hide things in.

Wedgli is a small site with practical guides for everyday life. Our core is organizing small homes: apartments, rentals, and studios where you want your space to work harder, without a renovation budget or owning the walls you live in.

Most organizing advice online assumes a house, a garage, and no landlord. We write for the rest of us: renters, students, and small-space dwellers who need ideas that fit an actual studio apartment, an actual security deposit, and an actual weekend.

Who this site is for

  • Renters who can't drill into walls or make permanent changes
  • Students living in dorms or shared apartments
  • Anyone in a studio, small bedroom, or one-closet home
  • People who want ideas that don't require a truck-sized haul from a big-box store

What you'll find here

Our main topic is small-space organizing: closets, kitchens, bedrooms, entryways, bathrooms, and budget-friendly picks. Every guide focuses on things you can do without permanent changes, and we write with small square footage in mind.

We also publish practical guides on everyday tools and offers, such as student AI deals, with links to each company's official page and a clear note on the catches. We try to be specific rather than generic. Instead of "declutter your closet," we aim for exact steps, exact product types, and clear facts you can check.

Our approach

Before publishing a guide, we check it against these questions:

  • Does it survive a security deposit? For organizing guides, nothing that requires drilling, painting, or altering a rented space.
  • Does it fit a real budget? We flag low-cost options alongside pricier ones.
  • Does it actually fit a small space? No advice that secretly assumes a spare room or garage.
  • Is the source official? For prices and offers, we link the company's own page, and we say so when we could not confirm something.

Where we're headed

We're still growing, and new guides are added over time. Upcoming topics include seasonal storage, moving-day organizing, small-space furniture picks, and more everyday guides. If there's a space you're struggling to organize, we'd love to hear about it. Visit our contact page to send a suggestion.
